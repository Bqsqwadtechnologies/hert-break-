# BQ Sqwad AI Promo Video & Companion Links

1. Place the promo video in `app/src/main/res/raw/bqsqwad_ai_promo.mp4`.
2. Format: MP4, H.264 video, AAC audio, portrait 1080x1920 or landscape 1280x720, 15-30 seconds, under 15 MB.
3. Links are configured in `config/companion.properties` (`WEB_URL`, `STORE_URL`, `PACKAGE`).
