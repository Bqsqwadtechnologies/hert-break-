Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Heartbreak Healer is published by BQ Sqwad Technologies. We built this app so your healing stays private.

What stays on your device

Your nickname, breakup date, goals, journal entries, unsent letters, mood check-ins, streaks, hearts balance and all other personal content are stored only on your device. We do not run user accounts and we do not upload this data to our servers.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers (including the advertising ID), IP address or approximate location, ad interactions, and diagnostics to deliver, measure, and protect advertising. Google processes this information under its own privacy terms. You can reset or limit the advertising ID in your device settings. Where required, Google UMP provides the applicable consent choices before ads are requested.

Ads may appear in clearly separated banner or native-ad placements on general screens and on the footer of selected private feature landing/list screens, including Journal, Unsent Letters, Memories, Triggers, No-Contact, and Settings. Ads are not embedded in journal or letter editors, private-entry composition fields, Urge SOS, or support information. The app does not send the text of your journal entries, letters, memories, or triggers to AdMob for ad targeting.

Permissions

  • Internet – to load ads
  • Notifications – optional daily reminders you control
  • Biometric / fingerprint – optional lock for the journal

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We ask users to confirm they are 13 or older during onboarding.

Your rights

Because data stays on your device you can delete everything at any time with the “Clear all data” option in Settings. That permanently removes local content.
