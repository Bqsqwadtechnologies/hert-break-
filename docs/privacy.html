Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Heartbreak Healer is published by BQ Sqwad Technologies. The app is designed to keep journal entries, letters, check-ins and other personal reflections in private app storage on your device. Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform process limited device and advertising information as described below.

What stays on your device

Your nickname, breakup date, goals, journal entries, unsent letters, mood check-ins, streaks, hearts balance and all other personal content are stored only on your device. We do not run user accounts and we do not upload this data to our servers.

Heart Rebuild Journey

The Heart Rebuild Journey data (your answers, completed days, daily checklist status, and preferences) stays strictly on your device in private app storage. It is never uploaded or transmitted to our servers or any third party.

Daily reminders and celebration notifications for the Heart Rebuild Journey are generated locally on your device by Android WorkManager.

BQ Sqwad AI house promotion and optional rewards

Heartbreak Healer may show an optional, clearly labeled “Ad · BQ Sqwad AI” house-promotion video for our sister product. It may be offered directly as a partner spotlight or shown as a fallback when a Google AdMob rewarded ad is unavailable. The house video is our own promotional content, not a Google-served ad. You choose whether to watch it; declining, skipping or closing it does not block normal app use.

When a screen clearly offers 15 Hearts for watching the house video, the 15 Hearts are added only after the video reaches its playback-completion event. If the video is offered to unlock specific premium content instead, the content named before playback is unlocked only after the video completes. Skipping, closing early, playback failure or displaying a static end card does not earn the reward or unlock the content. Some partner-spotlight offers unlock all four premium soundscapes; another unlocks the premium deep-breathwork session. The available reward is described on the screen before you opt in. Hearts and feature-unlock flags are stored locally on your device.

Choosing a clearly labeled “Learn more” or “Proceed with Companion” action opens BQ Sqwad AI outside Heartbreak Healer (the installed app, app store listing, or website). Android checks whether the single configured companion package can be launched so the app can choose the appropriate destination. This check is performed on-device; we do not collect or transmit the result. Once opened, the BQ Sqwad AI privacy policy applies. We do not transfer any Heartbreak Healer journal, letter, mood or journey content to BQ Sqwad AI.

Crash diagnostics

If the app closes unexpectedly, it saves one diagnostic report in private app storage. The report can include the time, Android version, device manufacturer and model, thread name and technical exception stack trace. Heartbreak Healer does not automatically upload this report. It is shared only if you choose to copy it and send it to support, and you can dismiss and clear it from the on-screen prompt.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers (including the advertising ID), IP address or approximate location, ad interactions, and diagnostics to deliver, measure, and protect advertising. Google processes this information under its own privacy terms. You can reset or limit the advertising ID in your device settings. Where required, Google UMP provides the applicable consent choices before ads are requested.

Ads may appear in clearly separated banner or native-ad placements on general screens and on the footer of selected private feature landing/list screens, including Journal, Unsent Letters, Memories, Triggers, No-Contact, and Settings. Ads are not embedded in journal or letter editors, private-entry composition fields, Urge SOS, or support information. The app does not send the text of your journal entries, letters, memories, or triggers to AdMob for ad targeting.

Permissions

  • Internet – to load ads and open external links you choose
  • Notifications – optional daily reminders you control
  • Vibration – optional tactile feedback
  • Biometric – Android's system biometric prompt can be used for the journal lock; the app does not access or store biometric templates

Children

Heartbreak Healer is not directed at children under 13. During onboarding, users must confirm that they are at least 13 years old before continuing. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can help address the concern.

Your rights

Because data stays on your device you can delete everything at any time with the “Clear all data” option in Settings. That permanently removes local content.
